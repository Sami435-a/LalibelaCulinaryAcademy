About the Academy

Trained on tradition,
built for the modern kitchen.

Lalibela Heritage Culinary Academy was founded to preserve and elevate Ethiopian heritage cooking, while equipping students with the professional standards demanded by today's kitchens.

Lalibela Highlands, Amhara Region

Named for the rock-hewn churches of Lalibela, the academy treats cooking as an inherited craft, one carried forward through precision, patience, and respect for ingredients native to this land.

Every student trains under working chefs, learning heritage Ethiopian technique alongside the structure and discipline of international culinary practice. The result is a chef who can run a professional kitchen anywhere, without losing the flavor of where they come from.
